## 1. Purpose of Dual / Staged Licensing

`Echo-memory-fabricks` separates open, verifiable specifications (`L1`: `specs/`, `docs/`, `README.md` under `Apache-2.0`) from executable runtime modules (`L2`: `src/`, `python/`, `mcp/`, `bin/`, `skills/`, `ui/`, `tests/` under the Source-Available Non-Competing & Verification Grant in `LICENSE`).

Because 100% sole copyright ownership is retained by the Copyright Holder (external pull requests into `L2` paths are not merged without an explicit written Copyright Assignment / CLA), any module or subset of `Echo-memory-fabricks` can be licensed flexibly across engagement structures:

1. **Commercial & Enterprise Production Licenses**: Custom closed-source embedding, SaaS/API deployment, or OEM redistribution.
2. **Solutions Consulting & Private Contracting ("Schedule A — Pre-Existing Background IP")**: All modules listed in `specs/background-ip-ledger.json` constitute **Pre-Existing Background Intellectual Property** conceived, engineered, and reduced to practice independently prior to any external client engagement. 3. **Senior Technical Advisory ("Tools of the Trade")**: Use of internal or companion memory-fabric tooling during architectural review, verification auditing, or strategic advisory engagements does not transfer software rights or implied licenses unless expressly scheduled in writing.
4. **R&D Grants & Government Contracting (`Private-Expense Provenance`)**: Modules sealed in `specs/background-ip-ledger.json` are developed exclusively at private expense prior to any external contract or grant award, preserving eligibility for Restricted Computer Software / Limited Rights designations under applicable procurement regulations.
